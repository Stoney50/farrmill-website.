Who we are

Built on expertise.
Run to a high standard.

I'm Jeremy Vavra. In 2019 I purchased Farrmill Pest Control from its founder, Garry Millar, who built the business over 25 years. That history doesn't sit on a shelf — it's in how the work gets done.

Farrmill isn't a franchise, and it isn't a call centre dispatching whoever's free. Every job that goes out under this name is held to the same standard it always has been — a licensed technician who does the work properly, explains what they're doing, and stands behind the result.

Most of our work still comes from referrals and repeat clients — people who've trusted Farrmill for years and pass our name on. That's the reputation we protect on every single job.
