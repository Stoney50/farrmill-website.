Our WA speciality

Termite Management

Perth is one of the highest-risk termite regions in Australia. We provide the full spectrum of protection — from detailed timber-pest inspections to complete treatment and prevention systems.

  • Timber-pest inspections

    Comprehensive inspections to Australian Standard AS 3660, with a clear written report.

  • Chemical barriers & reticulation

    Treated zones and reticulation systems that protect the structure long-term.

  • Baiting & monitoring systems

    Ongoing colony elimination and early-warning stations around your property.

  • Pre-purchase reports

    Buying a home? Know exactly what you're walking into before you sign.

Rodent Control

Rats and mice managed with secure, tamper-resistant baiting, plus exclusion and proofing so they can't get back in. Essential before winter, when rodents head indoors.

Spiders & Wasps

Redbacks, whitetails and webbing spiders treated around the home, plus safe removal of wasp and European wasp nests from eaves, sheds and entertaining areas.

Flea Treatments

End-of-lease and rental vacate flea treatments, with a certificate of treatment for tenants and property managers. Effective indoor and outdoor knockdown to break the flea life cycle.

Cockroach Treatments

German and Australian cockroaches targeted with gel baits and residual sprays in kitchens, subfloors and voids where they breed.

Ant Management

From nuisance black ants to coastal brown ants and white-footed ants — colony-level treatments, not just surface sprays.
