The warning signs

Would you spot the signs?

Termites work silently, often for years, before any visible damage. Watch for:

  • Mud tubes on walls or footings
  • Hollow or "papery" sounding timber
  • Doors or windows suddenly sticking
  • Discarded wings near windows

Seen any of these? Don't disturb them — call us first.
