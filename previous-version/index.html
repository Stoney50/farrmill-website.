Frequently asked questions

In Perth's high-risk termite environment, the Australian Standard recommends a professional inspection at least once every 12 months — more often for higher-risk properties. It's a small annual cost compared to the tens of thousands termite damage can cause.
While pesticides are poisons, the toxicity varies. We apply our chemical treatments in a way that keeps you, your family and pets safe. There are also non-chemical, natural-based treatments available, but they do not last as long.
We're based in Gosnells and focus on a 50 km radius, but we regularly travel further for established clients — from Moora down to Donnybrook. Give us a call and we'll let you know straight away.
