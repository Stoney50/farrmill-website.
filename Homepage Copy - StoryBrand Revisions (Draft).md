# Homepage Copy — StoryBrand Revisions (Draft)

From the board session, 21 July 2026 (Miller + Dib). Applies StoryBrand thinking to the existing `homepage-copy.md` draft. These are additions/edits to carry into whichever build ends up live — this one, or a fresh start.

---

## 1. Hero headline — options

Current: "Pests sorted. Properly." — service-first, not problem-first. Three options that lead with the customer's situation instead:

- **A:** "Pests dealt with. Damage stopped before it costs you." (leads with stakes)
- **B:** "Termites and pests don't wait. Neither do we." (urgency without being pushy)
- **C:** Keep "Pests sorted. Properly." as-is, but let "Properly" carry more weight by having the plan section (below) explain what "properly" actually means — thorough inspection, straight answer, no upsell.

Jeremy's call on tone. C is the lowest-risk option if he'd rather not rewrite the hero at all.

---

## 2. New section — "The Plan" (insert after Hero, before Trust Strip)

This doesn't exist anywhere in the current copy. StoryBrand wants three visible, dead-simple steps so a worried visitor can see exactly what happens next.

**Section heading (H2):** How it works

**Step 1**
- Heading: Book your inspection or quote
- Body: Tell us what's going on — or if you're not sure, we'll come check.

**Step 2**
- Heading: Get a straight answer
- Body: We find what's actually there, explain the risk in plain terms, and recommend what it needs — not what's easiest to sell.

**Step 3**
- Heading: Stay protected
- Body: One-off treatment or an ongoing program — whichever fits. Either way, you stop having to think about it.

---

## 3. Stakes line — Termite Management card

Current body copy states what the service does, but never what happens if it's skipped. One line added:

**Card 2 — Termite Management (revised body):**
Inspections, barrier treatments, reticulation systems, and baiting programs — we find them, assess the risk, and recommend the right solution for your property. Termite damage is often invisible until it's expensive — regular inspection is the cheapest insurance your house has.

---

## Notes

- No changes suggested to the Trust Strip or About section — both already do the "guide has authority" job StoryBrand asks for.
- Transitional CTA (a lower-commitment "not sure if you've got a problem?" offer) deliberately left out — that's pest-library/phase-2 territory, consistent with keeping this build simple first.
- If the site gets rebuilt from scratch, these three pieces (headline choice, Plan section, stakes line) are the parts worth carrying over regardless of the new design — everything else in `homepage-copy.md` still holds up.
