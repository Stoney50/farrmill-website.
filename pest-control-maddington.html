What we see most in Maddington

Homes, river flats and industry side by side

Maddington sits between our Gosnells base and the Canning River, with established homes that have stood for decades alongside the light-industrial area around Kelvin Road. Older homes, river flats and industry this close together mean we see a bit of everything here.

Termites in older homes

Homes that have been standing for decades may never have had their termite protection renewed. Thermal imaging and moisture meters help us find activity hidden inside walls.

Rats near the river and yards

Rats move between the river foreshore, industrial yards and back gardens. Tamper-resistant bait stations and sealing entry points keep them out of roofs and sheds.

Business pest programs

Workshops, warehouses and food businesses in the Maddington industrial area get scheduled, documented programs built around how the site actually runs.
