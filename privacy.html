Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal information.

Last updated: 24 July 2026

Farrmill Pest Control (ABN 78 233 135 833) ("Farrmill", "we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your personal information. As a small business, we follow the Australian Privacy Principles under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) as a matter of good practice.

What information we collect

We only collect information that we need to respond to your enquiry and provide our services. This may include:

  • Your name and the contact details you give us (phone number, email address);
  • The property address and suburb where work is required;
  • Details about the pest problem, service requested and preferred timing;
  • Records of the work we carry out, including inspection findings, treatments and reports;
  • Any other information you choose to provide when you contact us.

How we collect it

We collect this information directly from you — through our online booking form, by phone, by email, or in person when we attend your property. We do not purchase personal information from third parties.

Why we collect and how we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and provide a quote;
  • To schedule, carry out and follow up on pest control services;
  • To send you appointment reminders and notifications about your service;
  • To prepare inspection reports and service records;
  • To manage invoicing and payments, including invoice and account notifications sent through our accounting software;
  • To meet our legal, insurance and record-keeping obligations.

We do not send marketing. Any messages we send are about your appointments, service reminders, or your account. You can choose whether we contact you by email or text message, and you can change your preference — or ask us to stop — at any time. If you no longer wish to be a customer, just let us know and we will archive your details.

Payments

We accept cash, bank transfer, mobile EFTPOS at your appointment, and online payments through our payment provider, Stripe (which supports credit and debit cards and Apple Pay / Google Pay). When you pay online or by card, your details are entered directly with the payment provider and processed securely — we do not see or store your card or banking details. If a refund is ever needed, any details we ask for are used for that one purpose only and are not retained.

Disclosure of your information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it only where necessary — for example, with trusted service providers who help us operate (such as our website host, email provider, payment provider and accounting software), or where we are required or authorised to do so by law. Where a property manager or real estate agent has arranged work on your behalf, relevant service details may be shared with them. Some of these providers may store information outside Australia.

How long we keep your information

We keep your personal information only for as long as we reasonably need it. As a general rule, we retain client records for six years after our last contact with you. If there has been no further contact after that period, we securely remove your information to help protect your privacy.

Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold from misuse, loss and unauthorised access, including secure (HTTPS) transmission of form submissions and restricting access to your information to those who need it.

Our website and cookies

We host our fonts on our own website, so loading our pages does not send your details to a third-party font service. We do not use our website to collect personal information beyond what you voluntarily submit through the booking form.

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site so we can improve it. This service may set cookies and collect information such as your IP address, device and browser type, and the pages you visit. This information is used in aggregate and is not used to personally identify you. You can control or block cookies through your browser settings, and you can opt out of Google Analytics using Google's browser opt-out tool.

Access and correction

You can ask to access the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it if it is inaccurate. Contact us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Complaints

If you have a concern about how we have handled your personal information, please contact us first so we can try to resolve it. If you are not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be available on this page, with the "last updated" date shown above.

Contact us

For any privacy questions or requests, contact us at info@farrmill.com.au or on 0411 240 281.
